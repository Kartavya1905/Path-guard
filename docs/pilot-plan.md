# Pilot plan (human testing: **pending**)

No human testing has taken place yet. This plan describes the first small pilot. Results go in the table at the end only after real sessions happen.

## Who

Six to nine participants: designers or project managers who plan cross-team work. Recruit two or three per workflow below. It's free, run as volunteer sessions, with no paid tools.

## Three workflows

| # | Workflow | Starting point → goal | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Checkout change | "Checkout change approved" → "Reliable launch with verified checkout behavior" | Uses the checkout rule template and the seeded example |
| 2 | Design-system migration | "New component library chosen" → "All product screens migrated" | Handoffs between design and engineering, approvals, deadline pressure |
| 3 | Event or campaign launch | "Campaign brief signed off" → "Campaign live on launch day" | Many stakeholders, vendor dependencies, a fixed date |

## Session (45 minutes, one person at a time)

1. **Before (5 min):** the participant lists, on paper, the risks they already see for their workflow.
2. **Model (15 min):** they enter start, goal and their real tasks, generate the map, and review the suggestions.
3. **Explore (10 min):** worst cases, one cascade, and estimates for two or three decisions (hypothetical is fine).
4. **Compare (10 min):** save a baseline, create one mitigation variant, and read the comparison.
5. **Debrief (5 min):** the three questions below.

## What we measure

| Measure | How | Success looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Found an actionable risk they had missed | Compare their "before" list with risks they accepted or added | They name at least one accepted risk not on the before list, and a concrete action for it |
| Can explain why a risk is ranked where it is | Ask "why is #1 above #2?" with "Why this rank" hidden | Their explanation mentions impact first, then worst-case likelihood |
| Can explain what produces the mitigation delta | Ask "what assumption makes the variant better?" | They name the changed estimate(s) and the assumption behind them, not just "the mitigation" |

We also note time to first generated map, any wrong turn, and every place the wording confused them.

## Data handling

- Use the local install, or the hosted demo with its per-browser privacy and 7-day expiry.
- Participants shouldn't enter real confidential project data.
- Notes are kept without names.
